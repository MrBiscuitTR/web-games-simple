Texas Hold'em Rules

Hand Rankings (high → low)

  1. Royal Flush
  2. Straight Flush
  3. Four of a Kind
  4. Full House
  5. Flush
  6. Straight
  7. Three of a Kind

  9. One Pair
  10. High Card

Gameplay

  • Each player gets 2 hole cards.
  • 5 community cards revealed: Flop (3), Turn (1), River (1).
  • Best 5-card hand from 7 cards wins.
  • Blinds rotate each round. Big blind = 2× Small blind.
  • Actions: Fold, Check/Call, Raise/Bet.
  • All-in creates side pots when needed.

Play Money Only

  • All chips are valueless virtual tokens with no cash value. The ¤ sign is a generic placeholder and is not any real currency.
  • There is no real-money play, betting or wagering of any kind.
  • Nothing can be bought, deposited, withdrawn, won or cashed out.
  • Chips reset at the end of a session and cannot be traded or transferred.
