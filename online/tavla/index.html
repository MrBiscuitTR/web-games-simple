Tavla Rules

Erkek Tavlası

  • Goal: Bear off all 15 checkers before your opponent.
  • Dice: Roll two dice. Doubles give 4 moves.
  • Hitting: Land on an opponent's lone checker (blot) to send it to the bar.
  • Bar: Must re-enter from the bar before making other moves.
  • Blocking: 2+ opponent pieces on a point — can't land there.
  • Mars: Win with opponent bearing none = 2pts; opponent still on your home board/bar = 3pts (Katmerli Mars). Normal win = 1pt.

Kız Tavlası

  • No hitting, no blocking. Race to bear off all pieces first.
